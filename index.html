AI-Fluent at Work

Use AI to work faster, and check it so you can stand behind your work

A self-paced programme for early-career professionals aged 18–25 across Africa. Five weeks, about three to four hours a week, built for phones and busy lives.

  • 5 weeks + 1 buffer
  • Fully asynchronous
  • Phone-first

The programme

Five weeks, one habit employers value

By the end, learners can:

  1. Decide where AI adds value in a task and where their own judgement must lead
  2. Direct AI with clear context to get a usable first draft
  3. Evaluate and improve AI output, then accept, revise or reject it
  4. Use AI responsibly, protecting confidential information and disclosing AI use appropriately
  5. Produce career-ready work with AI to a professional standard
  1. Week 1

    Where AI fits in your career

    Map your target role’s tasks: what to hand to AI, what to keep.

  2. Week 2

    Directing AI well

    Context, audience and constraints for a strong first draft.

  3. Week 3

    Making AI output good This lesson

    The 5-Point Check: facts, figures, sources, tone and local fit.

  4. Week 4

    Responsible AI on real work

    Three African workplace scenarios, confidentiality and disclosure.

  5. Week 5

    Show your skills

    Signature project: the AI-Fluent Work Sample, a raw AI draft, the improved version and notes on what changed.

Design notes

  • Explore, notice, understand, apply: the four activities build the skill in that order.
  • Phone-first: one column, large tap targets, no dragging.
  • No facilitator needed: every answer gets instant feedback that names the check.
  • Accessible: works by keyboard, feedback is written in words as well as colour, and both light and dark modes are designed.
  • Production assumption: each activity maps to an H5P type (Branching Scenario, Mark the Words, Drag and Drop, Single Choice Set). If H5P isn’t available, the same logic runs as simple web components like these.
